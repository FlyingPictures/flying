What is the difference between shared and private?

On a shared flight, you share the basket with other passengers. On a private flight, the basket is exclusively for your group and the pilot. Filter the experiences to compare both options.

Do all packages include transportation and breakfast?

No. Essential Flight does not include a full breakfast or transportation. Other packages have different inclusions: open the details to see exactly what is included in your choice.

Is the price per person or per package?

Essential Flight and Flight + Transport + Pyramids show adult and child rates. The other experiences show a package price. Our team will confirm the included guests and your booking total.

How do I check dates or book?

The availability button opens a conversation with your package name. Share your date, number of adults and children and any special requests. Our team will confirm availability and the next steps.

What happens if the weather does not allow a flight?

Flights depend on weather conditions. Before booking, ask our team about the rescheduling, cancellation and refund terms that apply to your experience.
